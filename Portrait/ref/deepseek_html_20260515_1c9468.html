① Adaptive CE Weighting

LCE* = – 1⁄N Σt η(s) · w[t] · log P(labelt)

② FCP: Format & EOS Penalty

LFCP = 1⁄|Mthink| Σt Mthink[t] · max(P(EOS|t) − δ, 0)
Penalizes the model whenever it assigns high probability to the EOS token inside the <think> segment. This prevents early termination and encourages full reasoning chains before the final answer.
