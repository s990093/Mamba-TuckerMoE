PROBLEMS — Deployment Bottlenecks

1. Attention O(N²·d) — step t scans all t–1 past tokens; 16K seq = 256× cost of early steps

2. KV Cache O(N) — 30-layer Transformer @16K = ~1.4 GiB; exceeds Apple Silicon 16 GB headroom

3. MoE weight volume E× — 8 experts × 1536 × 6144 = 75.5M per projection; per-expert SVD ignores cross-expert redundancy

SOLUTIONS — Hybrid Mamba-TuckerMoE

1. Mamba-3 SSM O(1) decode state — 9.0 MiB fixed, independent of sequence length

2. 4:1 Hybrid ratio — 80% KV reduction; only 6 Transformer layers @16K = 288 MiB

3. TuckerMoE joint 3-way decomp. — 82.87% compression; shared U²,U³ jointly optimized across all experts
